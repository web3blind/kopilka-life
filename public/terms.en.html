What this service is

Life Harbor is a gentle personal tracker of small actions that support your day: sleep, movement, food and water, joy, social contact, time with family, rest and other simple check-ins.

LIFE is not money

The LIFE unit is a gamified and motivational measure within the app. It is not a currency, not a loyalty programme, not electronic money and not a promise of material benefit.

Care agreement

A weekly care agreement is a personal promise to yourself. If the user specifies an amount for a care fund, the app does not hold money, accept payments or make transfers. This is only a text reminder.

User responsibility

  • the user decides which entries to add;
  • users should not put passwords, bank details, confidential medical information or other sensitive information in notes;
  • the service does not replace a doctor, psychologist, financial adviser or legal advice.

Limits

Each quick action for adding LIFE can be marked no more than once per local calendar day of the user. This keeps Life Harbor a calm diary rather than a race.

Public links

The user can share a public profile link. It shows only summary figures and referral information. You cannot manage someone else’s profile through a public link.

The service may evolve: wording, features and rules may be refined. The current version of the terms is available on this page.
